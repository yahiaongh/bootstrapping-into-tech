Privacy notice

This website may use Umami analytics to understand aggregate traffic and improve its learning materials.

What may be collected

When the site owner activates Umami, the website can collect pageviews, referrer and campaign information, device/browser information where available, and custom learning interactions such as PDF-link clicks, table-of-contents clicks, practice activities, quiz completion, scroll milestones, and future-topic options.

The custom event code does not intentionally send names, email addresses, student IDs, exercise answers, or other user-entered personal information.

Analytics status

In the delivered project, the tracking script remains a commented template until the site owner adds the actual Umami script URL and Website ID. If it remains disabled, custom events do not transmit to Umami. The site works without analytics.

Questions and legal requirements

The site owner should identify the operator and add an appropriate contact method if needed, and review the requirements that apply to the deployment location and audience. Using Umami does not automatically guarantee compliance with every privacy law.
